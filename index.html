FAQ

Common questions

How do I lock in my appointment?

Book your service and time, send your deposit ($20 for Retwist + Style / All 3, $50 for Reattachments) via Faith’s Cash App, Venmo, or Zelle, and upload your screenshot. Faith verifies it and confirms your appointment.

Where is the studio located?

Faith works out of her private home studio in Central Phoenix, AZ. For privacy, the exact address is shared once your appointment is confirmed.

How does reattachment pricing work?

1–5 locs is $10/loc, 6–10 locs is $8/loc, 11–20 locs starts at $100, 21+ locs starts at $150, and a Full-Head Reattachment (wash included) starts at $250. You’ll share your approximate loc count when booking and send photos beforehand.

How do mobile visits work?

Choose the Mobile Visit booking option when scheduling. Travel fee is +$20 if you’re under 30 minutes from Central Phoenix, or +$35 if over 30 minutes away.
