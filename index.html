How the calendar & texts would work

Proposal · needs Faith's choice

Every booking from the site lands on one calendar, so she can't get double-booked. The same booking then triggers the texts above.

  • Option A: keep GlossGenius. GlossGenius stays her calendar and keeps sending reminders. It already includes unlimited reminder texts. The site links to her GlossGenius booking, or blocks the same times.
  • Option B: site booking + Google Calendar. The site takes the required Stripe deposit and puts the appointment on her Google Calendar. Texts go out from a business number on her own Twilio account, which needs A2P 10DLC carrier registration (about $4 + $15 one-time, $2/month, plus a small per-text fee).
